**🍔FOOD DELIVERY PERFORMANCE DASHBOARD**

**📊Project Overview**

This project analyzes a 200-record food delivery dataset to understand delivery performance and the factors associated with delivery time.

The analysis was carried out using Microsoft Excel, with the results presented through an interactive dashboard.

The project focuses on delivery demand, delivery time, distance, traffic conditions, weather, delivery mode, and regional performance.

**🎯Project Objective**

The main objective was to analyze food delivery routes and identify patterns that may affect delivery efficiency and delivery time.

The project explores questions such as:
* How does delivery distance relate to delivery time?
* How does route length affect delivery time?
* Which regions have the highest number of deliveries?
* How do traffic conditions relate to delivery time?
* Does weather affect delivery time?
* How does delivery mode compare in terms of average delivery time?

**🔍Key Findings**

One of the strongest relationships identified in the analysis was between delivery distance and delivery time.

Average delivery time increased substantially as delivery distance increased:

Distance Group	  Average Delivery Time
0–3 km	          11.7 min
3–6 km	          28.2 min
6–9 km	          50.9 min
9–12 km	          71.1 min

These results show that longer delivery routes were generally associated with longer delivery times in this dataset.

**💼 Business Problem**
What factors are affecting delivery time, and how can the company improve it's delivery efficiency? 
This project analyzes delivery data to identify patterns in delivery time across distance, route length, traffic conditions, weather, delivery mode, and geographic region.

**🔍 Key Insights**
* Delivery distance and time increased together: Average delivery time rose from approximately 11.7 minutes for deliveries of 0–3 km to 71.1 minutes for deliveries of 9–12 km.
* Regional delivery volume varied: The South recorded the highest number of deliveries (54), while the East recorded the lowest (28).
* Weather differences were observed: Cloudy conditions had the highest average delivery time at approximately 49.94 minutes in this dataset.
* Traffic levels showed different average delivery times: Medium traffic recorded the lowest average delivery time at approximately 41.62 minutes, while Low traffic recorded the highest at approximately 47.05 minutes.

**💡 Recommendations**
  
Based on the analysis, the following actions could help improve delivery operations:
1. **Optimize delivery routes**  
   Since distance and route length have strong relationships with delivery time, optimizing routes could help reduce unnecessary travel time.
2. **Monitor longer-distance orders**  
   Orders covering longer distances should be identified early so that realistic delivery times can be communicated to customers.
3. **Review regional operations**  
   Delivery volume varies considerably by region, so resources and delivery capacity could be adjusted according to regional demand.
5. **Use data to improve delivery planning**  
   Regular analysis of delivery time, distance, traffic, and route data could help the business identify inefficiencies and improve operational planning.
